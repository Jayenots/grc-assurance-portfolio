# Assessor Workflow

This workflow defines the standard review approach used across the portfolio.

## 1. Define Scope
- Identify the system, process, service, or control environment being assessed.
- Define assessment boundaries.
- Identify owners and relevant stakeholders.
- Record assumptions and exclusions.

## 2. Identify Applicable Requirements and Controls
- Identify the governing framework, policy, contractual requirement, or control objective.
- Map each requirement to the relevant control.
- Record the source of the requirement.

## 3. Define Evidence Expectations
For each control, document:
- Evidence required
- Evidence owner
- Period covered
- Expected source
- Expected level of detail

## 4. Request and Review Evidence
- Confirm evidence is complete and relevant.
- Confirm the evidence covers the correct scope and period.
- Identify missing or insufficient evidence.
- Do not treat evidence presence alone as proof of control effectiveness.

## 5. Test Control Design
Determine whether the control, as designed, is capable of meeting the stated requirement.

Possible results:
- Effective
- Partially Effective
- Ineffective
- Not Yet Testable

## 6. Test Operating Effectiveness
Where applicable:
- Define the population.
- Validate population completeness.
- Select a defensible sample.
- Perform the test.
- Record exceptions.

## 7. Document Findings
Each finding should include:
- Requirement or control
- Observation
- Evidence
- Risk
- Severity
- Recommendation

## 8. Track Remediation
- Assign an owner.
- Define corrective action.
- Set a target date.
- Track status.

## 9. Retest
- Review updated evidence.
- Repeat the relevant test.
- Confirm whether the original issue is resolved.

## 10. Issue Final Conclusion
The final conclusion should state:
- What was assessed
- What evidence was reviewed
- What testing was performed
- What passed
- What remains open
- Residual risk
- Final assurance conclusion
